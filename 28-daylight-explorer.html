What is calculated

The yellow marker is the subsolar point, where the Sun is overhead. The blue curve is the geometric day/night terminator. Sunrise and sunset use the conventional −0.833° solar elevation to approximate refraction and the solar disc radius. Twilight boundaries use −6°, −12° and −18°. Polar day/night is reported when no crossing exists.

Sources & assumptions

Method reference: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory Solar Calculation Details. NOAA says its calculator is no longer actively maintained; its equations are based on Jean Meeus and theoretical sunrise/sunset accuracy is about one minute between ±72° latitude and about ten minutes outside that range, while atmospheric conditions can change observed times. This map is an independent client-side implementation, not a NOAA product. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors. Rendering: Leaflet 1.9.4.
