What this means

The lines show hydrologic network connectivity in NHDPlusV2. “Upstream” means reaches connected toward contributing headwaters; “downstream” follows the modeled mainstem away from the selected reach. This is not a contaminant travel-time model, flood forecast, discharge estimate, or proof that water is currently flowing through every displayed segment.
