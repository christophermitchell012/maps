How to read the map

The NASA VIIRS Black Marble layer is a 2016 annual nighttime-light composite. Brighter areas are a proxy for upward-emitted/artificial light, not a direct measurement of sky brightness or a current light-pollution reading. Cloud cover is the nearest available NWS forecast-grid value. Moon illumination is calculated locally from the current date. The viewing rating uses cloud cover + Moon illumination only; visually inspect the Black Marble layer for local light context.

Viewing rating

Derived, not official. Rating = 100 − (0.70 × cloud %) − (0.30 × Moon illumination %). 80–100 Excellent, 60–79 Good, 40–59 Fair, below 40 Poor. Artificial light is deliberately not converted to a numeric score because this imagery is a historical radiance proxy, not calibrated sky brightness.
