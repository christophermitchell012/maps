How to read the map

NASA VIIRS Black Marble is a 2016 annual nighttime-light composite. Brighter areas are a proxy for artificial/upward-emitted light, not direct sky brightness or a current light-pollution reading. Cloud cover is the NWS forecast-grid value nearest approximately 10 PM local solar time. Moon illumination is calculated locally for that target time.

Selected-location context

Derived, not official. A map click still uses the lightweight cloud + Moon context score shown above. The 100-place ranking is separate: it uses numerical VNP46A4 radiance plus cloud, Moon altitude/illumination and astronomical-dark hours from the prebuilt snapshot.
