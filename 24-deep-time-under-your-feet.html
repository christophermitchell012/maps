What this means

The geology layer shows the mapped surface unit at the clicked location. It does not tell you what rock is at foundation, tunnel, aquifer, or well depth. Paleoposition is a plate-model reconstruction, not a measured historical coordinate. Model uncertainty grows deeper in time.
