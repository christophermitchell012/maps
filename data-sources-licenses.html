Map 26 · Dark Sky Tonight

U.S. Census Bureau · 2025 Gazetteer and Vintage 2025 population estimates

Candidate place names, coordinates and selection population

The 100-place ranking uses official 2025 Census Gazetteer place names and representative latitude/longitude coordinates. Candidate selection uses the Census Bureau Population Estimates Program Vintage 2025 national subcounty file and its July 1, 2025 POPESTIMATE2025 field to identify a highest-population incorporated place and, where a distinct one is available, a low-density incorporated place in each contiguous state/DC. Population is used to construct the representative candidate set, not as an ingredient in the Tonight Score.

  • 2025 Census Gazetteer Files
  • Vintage 2025 City and Town Population Totals
  • SUB-EST2025 file layout

The ranking covers only these 100 representative Census places. It does not claim to rank every location or observing site in the United States, and no Census Bureau endorsement is implied.

NASA VIIRS VNP46A4 Collection 2, 2024

Scientific radiance data

The ranking prototype uses the 2024 annual VNP46A4 Collection 2 product at build time. The Earthdata token used to access LAADS is a private build credential and is not included in public HTML, JavaScript or JSON.

Dataset citation supplied for the product:
NASA VIIRS Land Science Investigator-led Processing System. (2025). VIIRS/NPP Lunar BRDF-Adjusted Nighttime Lights Yearly L3 Global 15 arc second Linear Lat Lon Grid [Dataset]. NASA Level 1 and Atmosphere Archive and Distribution System Distributed Active Archive Center. doi:10.5067/VIIRS/VNP46A4.002.

MitchellCo reports the source radiance in nW/(cm²·sr). It is an upward-emitted/nighttime radiance measure, not a Bortle class and not a direct measurement of zenith sky brightness.

MitchellCo Tonight Score v2

For Map 26, only forecast-covered samples with Sun altitude at or below −18° count as astronomical darkness. The algorithm searches the next 24 hours for the best contiguous three-hour dark window. Its core score is 50% transformed annual VIIRS radiance, 35% NWS clear-sky percentage, and 15% Moon score. Moon interference uses illumination and altitude and becomes zero when the Moon is below the horizon. The final score is 90% of that best-window core plus 10% based on total forecast-covered astronomical-dark hours. This is a MitchellCo heuristic for comparative planning context, not an official astronomical forecast.

Release philosophy

When a source requires a specific notice, year, copyright statement, citation, or license link, MitchellCo retains it. If the required year is not known from the source data, publication is held rather than guessed. Sources with unresolved commercial-use or redistribution terms are excluded until cleared.
